A simple, open-source YouTube downloader for Android.
This app uses NewPipe Extractor to fetch downloadable YouTube streams

Features:

- Download as MP3 or MP4
- Embedded metadata and album art for MP3s
- Quick download mode or manual quality selection
- YouTube search with thumbnails, duration and channel name
- Download history
- Custom download destination via folder picker
- Share YouTube links directly from the YouTube app
- Dark theme
